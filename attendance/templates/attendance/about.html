ABOUT FACEATTEND

Smarter Attendance. Better Management.

FaceAttend is a modern Facial Recognition Attendance System designed to simplify student attendance using computer vision, automation and digital record management.

Our System

A Modern Approach to Student Attendance

Traditional attendance methods can be time-consuming and require manual record keeping. FaceAttend provides a smarter digital solution.

The system uses a camera to detect and recognize registered student faces. After successful recognition, the student's attendance is automatically recorded.

Student information, attendance records and system statistics can be managed through a centralized Django-based web application.

👁️

Intelligent Recognition

FaceAttend combines facial detection, face recognition and database management to create a complete automated attendance workflow.
